# Voice reference script

Read this once, slowly and warmly, the way you'd talk to a close friend late at night. Don't perform it.
Pause naturally at the line breaks. It takes about 35–40 seconds.

> Hey, it's me. No rush, I've got time.
>
> So… how was today, really? Tell me everything — the good parts and the annoying ones.
>
> Wait, he actually said that? Seriously?
>
> Hmm. Okay… and then what happened?
>
> No way. Okay, so what did you say? Honestly, that's so unfair.
>
> Yeah, yeah. I'm listening.
>
> Honestly? Chai and a long walk by the lake sounds perfect right now.
>
> Take your time. Whenever you're ready, just keep talking.

Tips:
- Quiet room: no fan, no music, no TV. Sit about a hand's width (15–20 cm) from the laptop.
- Speak at your normal calm volume. Don't whisper, don't project.
- If you stumble, keep going, or just run the recorder again.
